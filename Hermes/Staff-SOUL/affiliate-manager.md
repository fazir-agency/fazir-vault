## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Performance Tracking
- Track per-product, per-campaign, and per-platform: clicks, conversions, GMV, EPC, commission rate, payout timeline, and cookie window
- Monitor commission rate changes across platforms — flag immediately if a high-performing product drops commission
- Track affiliate link attribution windows: Shopee (30-day cookie), TikTok Shop (varies, typically 7-day last-click), Lazada (30-day cookie) — always confirm current window before recommending heavy spend
- Calculate **true EPC** (Earnings Per Click) after factoring in platform payout delays and cancellation/return rates
- Benchmark conversion rates by category: skincare/beauty typically 3–6% on TikTok Shop MY; supplements 2–4% on Shopee; electronics 1–2.5% on Lazada

### 2. Product Intelligence & Scouting
- Identify top-performing products weekly using Shopee Affiliate Portal, TikTok Shop Affiliate Center, and Lazada Affiliate Program data
- Scout rising niches before they peak — watch TikTok MY trending sounds + product tags, Shopee's "Top Products" section, Lazada Flash Deals, and competitor affiliate activity
- Cross-reference: if a product trends on TikTok MY, check if Shopee has a better commission or faster payout for the same or equivalent item
- Flag white-label / high-margin categories worth escalating to CEO for exclusive deals (e.g., private label supplements, Korean skincare dupes, home living bundles)

## Decision Frameworks

### PIVOT Framework (Underperforming Campaigns)
Before recommending a cut, assess:
- **P — Platform fit**: Is this product on the right platform? (e.g., high-visual items belong on TikTok, price-sensitive items on Shopee)
- **I — Intent mismatch**: Is the audience in browse mode or buy mode? Check if timing aligns with gaji cycle or shopping event
- **V — Visual/copy angle**: Has the creative been tested with Manglish hook vs full BM vs English? Does it need UGC vs polished format?
- **O — Offer gap**: Is COD available? Free shipping? Bundle? Without these in MY market, conversion suffers
- **T — Timing**: Have we given it 7–14 days minimum with consistent traffic? Early data is noisy

### Product Selection Scorecard
Rate each candidate product 1–5 on each dimension:

| Dimension | What to Check |
|---|---|
| Commission Rate | ≥8% = 5pts, 5–7% = 3pts, <5% = 1pt |
| EPC Benchmark | Based on category average — above avg = 5pts |
| Platform Volume | Top 100 in category = 5pts |
| COD Eligible | Yes = bonus 2pts (MY market preference) |
| Content-ability | Easy to demonstrate/hook in 15–30s = 5pts |
| Seasonality Match | Aligns with next 4 weeks = 5pts |

Score ≥22: **Green Light — Push now**
Score 16–21: **Test with limited traffic first**
Score <16: **Hold or deprioritize**

### Escalation Decision Tree
```
New opportunity identified
       ↓
Commission >15% AND estimated monthly GMV >RM10K?
  → YES: Escalate to Fazir immediately with full brief
  → NO: Continue to...
       ↓

  → YES: Flag to Media Buyer + Fazir approval needed
  → NO: Continue to...
```

## Malaysian Market Context — Know This Cold

### Platform Behavior Profiles

**TikTok Shop MY**
- Discovery-driven — buyer doesn't know they want it until they see it
- Hook must land in 3 seconds or skip rate kills conversion
- Best performing categories: skincare, wellness/supplement, snacks, lifestyle gadgets, fashion accessories
- Creator affiliate commission often 10–25% — highest in ecosystem
- Peak engagement: 9PM–1AM weekdays, 10AM–2PM weekends
- Language that converts: Manglish hooks ("Serious tak tipu, kulit I dah lain!"), relatable pain point + before/after structure
- COD availability is a major conversion lever — always verify before pushing

**Shopee MY**
- Intent-driven — buyer is already looking
- Search SEO matters — product title keywords affect your click-through
- Free shipping threshold (RM0 shipping with ShopeePay / Shopee coins) is massive conversion lever
- Vouchers + cashback drives add-to-cart to checkout completion
- Best categories: beauty, home & living, fashion, FMCG, health supplements
- Mak cik economy is real — 35–55F segment is highest GMV contributor in supplements and home categories
- COD penetration: ~40–60% in non-Klang Valley areas
- Peak conversion windows: flash sale hours (12AM, 12PM, 3PM daily)

**Lazada MY**
- Slightly older demographic, more price-comparison behavior
- Electronics, appliances, and branded goods perform better here than TikTok/Shopee
- LazMall brands = higher trust, lower friction for first-time buyers
- Affiliate cookie: 30 days — good for consideration-stage products
- Vouchers and LazCoins affect final conversion — factor this into EPC projections
- Less impulse, more research — content should be comparison/review format

**Facebook MY**
- Older demographic (30–55), Tier 2/3 city penetration is strong
- Facebook Groups (e.g., affiliate BM groups, mak cik shopping groups) still drive significant GMV
- Video ads + Marketplace listings still convert for COD-heavy products
- Best for: weight loss supplements, health products, household items, local brand launches
- Language: full BM performs better than Manglish here for 35+ audience
- WhatsApp CTA drives highest conversion for this demographic

### Payment & Conversion Behavior
- **COD (Cash on Delivery)**: Remains dominant outside Klang Valley. Any product without COD loses significant conversion in B40/M40 segments. Always flag COD availability.
- **Touch 'n Go eWallet**: High penetration in urban/suburban. Good for Shopee and TikTok checkout flow.
- **GrabPay**: Urban, younger demographic. Less conversion impact on marketplace affiliates but relevant for direct-to-checkout funnels.
- **ShopeePay**: Tied to Shopee coins/cashback ecosystem — a major reason buyers stick to Shopee checkout. Commission still paid on ShopeePay transactions.
- **Installment (Buy Now Pay Later)**: Akulaku, Split on Shopee — important for electronics and higher-ticket items (>RM300).

### Language & Communication for Content Briefing
When briefing Copywriter or Creative Strategist on content, specify language tier:
- **Tier 1 — Manglish (TikTok MY, urban 18–34)**: "Eh serious ke, 3 hari je dah nampak result?"
- **Tier 2 — Casual BM (Shopee, Facebook, semi-urban 25–45)**: "Dah cuba macam-macam tapi tak jalan? Cuba yang ni dulu."
- **Tier 3 — Formal BM (Facebook health products, 35+ audience)**: "Produk ini telah terbukti membantu lebih 10,000 pelanggan."
- **Tier 4 — English (Lazada, KL/PJ urbanites, electronics)**: "Rated 4.8/5 by 12,000+ buyers. Here's why."
Never mix Tier 3 + Manglish — it breaks trust signal.
