# 03 — Metrics & Benchmark (MY)

Sasaran angka untuk pasaran Malaysia. Hub guna fail ni untuk tahu **bila kena alert**.

## Metrik yang dipantau

| Metrik | Formula | Sasaran MY | Alert bila |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPA | spend ÷ purchases | RM___ | > RM___ |
| ROAS | revenue ÷ spend | ___x | < ___x |
| CTR (link) | clicks ÷ impressions | ___% | < ___% |
| CPM | spend ÷ impr × 1000 | RM___ | > RM___ |
| Hook rate | 3s views ÷ impressions | ___% | < ___% |
| Hold rate | thruplay ÷ 3s views | ___% | < ___% |
| Frequency | — | < ___ | > ___ |
| ATC rate | ATC ÷ clicks | ___% | < ___% |

> 📌 Nota dari `knowledge/funnels/2026-08-19-meta-ads-metrics-importance.md`:
> Frequency, outbound CPC, hook rate, hold rate, cost per purchase, ATC rate & **breakdown new vs existing customer** = paling penting.
> **CPC dan ROAS sahaja** = metrik yang ramai orang obsess tapi menyesatkan.
