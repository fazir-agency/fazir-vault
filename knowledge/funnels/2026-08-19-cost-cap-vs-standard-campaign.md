## Apa knowledge ni
Masalah bila run **standard campaign + cost-cap campaign** serentak — cost-cap campaign boleh jadi "retargeting tersembunyi" yang overpay customer sedia ada.

## Isi (dari audio)
- Contoh: standard campaign $1k/hari + cost-cap campaign $1k/hari pada $50 CPA.
- Standard campaign spend anyway (tak kira CPA) — dia drive top-of-funnel.
- Cost-cap campaign "selalu hit" $50 CPA sebab ia **menjadi retargeting** — ia target demand yang standard campaign dah create (lagi teruk kalau 1-day view enabled).
- Kesannya: CPA cost-cap campaign nampak bagus, tapi **incrementality rendah** — duit bazir pada existing customer, overpay.
- **Fix:** (1) matikan 1-day view, (2) set **exclusions** supaya cost-cap campaign hit NEW customers sahaja.

## Kenapa penting
Ini penjelasan kenapa "cost cap = CPA bagus" boleh jadi ilusi. Fazir boleh elak bazir duit dengan setup exclusions + matikan 1-day view.

## Macam mana nak guna
- Bila run cost-cap + standard serentak, sentiasa set exclusion (new customer) pada cost-cap campaign.
- Matikan 1-day view attribution untuk elak double-counting.
- Ukur incrementality, bukan CPA sahaja.
