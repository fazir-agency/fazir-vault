## Apa knowledge ni
Matthew ranking **elemen creative ad** ikut mana yang penting vs tak penting (untuk performance).

## Elemen creative — penting ke tak
| Elemen | Penting? |
|--------|----------|
| Problem-based talk | ✅ Penting |
| Scientific sales arguments / unique mechanisms | ✅✅ Sangat penting |
| Comparison dengan alternatif biasa | ✅ Penting |
| Direct CTA | ✅ Penting |
| Urgency / scarcity | ✅ Penting |
| Wajah celebrity | ✅ Penting |
| Sebut jumlah review | ✅ Penting |
| Giving discount | ❌ Tak penting (ikut product/niche) |
| Big logo untuk branding | ❌ Tak penting |
| Wajah founder | ❌ Tak penting |
| Cinema camera production | ❌ Tak penting |

## Kenapa penting
Kebanyakan brand buang duit pada benda yang tak penting (cinema production, logo besar, wajah founder) dan abaikan yang penting (unique mechanism, problem-based talk, urgency). Ini checklist untuk audit creative.

## Macam mana nak guna
- Audit ad client guna checklist ni — buang elemen "tak penting" yang bazir budget.
- Fokus production effort pada: unique mechanism, problem talk, comparison, urgency, social proof (review/celebrity).
- Jangan over-invest pada cinematic production — tak drive conversion.
