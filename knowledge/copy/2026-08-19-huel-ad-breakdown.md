## Apa knowledge ni
Matthew breakdown satu ad **Huel** (meal replacement) dan ajar teknik storytelling/format untuk ad yang nampak organic.

## Teknik yang dia ajar (urutan ad)
1. **Absurd comedy hook** — "He doesn't eat solid meals, only Huel every day" (siapa yang buat begitu?).
2. **Open loop** — mula cerita customer sebenar ("one of our customers, James").
3. **MetaGlass POV format** — format organic yang tengah blowing up, blend masuk feed.
4. **Specificity = believable** — "Amazon Ultra 260km through Amazon rainforest" (setting ekstrem).
5. **Social proof tersirat** — "everyone said he can't finish" → imply dia berjaya.
6. **Anchor performance → baru benefits** — selepas extreme performance, baru masuk 40g protein/vitamin/fiber.
7. **Remove effort** — "one simple scoop" buat nutrition nampak mudah.
8. **Address pain point** — "days when healthy eating feels impossible."
9. **Guna own footage** (packaging/shipping) bukan stock — story reinforce benefits + brand.

## Kenapa penting
Ini framework untuk **story-driven ad yang nampak organic** (bukan "ad-like"). Format POV + customer story ni berkesan untuk brand consumer/supplement.

## Macam mana nak guna
- Untuk client supplement, guna hook absurd/comedy + customer story (bukan hard sell).
- Guna POV format + own footage (bukan stock) supaya blend dalam feed.
- Struktur: hook → open loop → specificity → anchor performance → benefits → pain point → CTA.
