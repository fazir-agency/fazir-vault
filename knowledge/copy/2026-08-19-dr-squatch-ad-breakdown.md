## Apa knowledge ni
Matthew breakdown satu ad **Dr. Squatch** (9 soaps + free dopp kit) dan ajar teknik copy/offer untuk lower-funnel ad.

## Teknik yang dia ajar (urutan ad)
1. **Hook lead dengan offer** — "Nine soaps + free dopp kit?" terus signal ini MOF/BOF ad.
2. **Refer brand name** — "Dr. Squatch just dropped..." target orang yang dah kenal brand (capture existing awareness).
3. **Naikkan perceived value** — "9 rugged bars... smelling like a legend for months" (bahasa ICP).
4. **GWP jadi main offer** — free limited edition dopp kit, checklist edit buat ia nampak lebih power dari main offer.
5. **Handle objection** — "not some flimsy throwaway bag" jawab objection GWP low quality.
6. **Value dulu, discount kemudian** — build perceived value → baru contrast dengan 39% off.
7. **Scarcity + urgency** — "limited edition, won't stick around, once sold out it's done."
8. **Humor** — break up salesy messaging.
9. **CTA** — implicit CTA ok, tapi direct CTA lebih baik.

## Kenapa penting
Ini framework copy untuk **lower-funnel offer ad** — bila auction dah saturated, kena maximize revenue per customer. Fazir boleh tiru struktur ni untuk client supplement/consumer brand.

## Macam mana nak guna
- Guna urutan ni untuk ad offer: hook=offer → perceived value → objection → discount → scarcity → CTA.
- Buat GWP nampak macam main offer (checklist edit).
- Untuk client yang dah ada awareness, guna lower-funnel ad macam ni untuk maximize revenue.
