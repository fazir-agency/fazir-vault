# Meta Ad Creative Tier List — Matthew Feng (51 Formats)

**Sumber:** Matthew Feng (@matthew.feng_) — Paid Ads Expert, generated $500M+ untuk DTC brands. Meta, Google & TikTok Ad Insights. Reel: `DZ71w_GqNkq` siri "What do you think about this tier list?" + video YouTube "I Ranked 51 Meta Ad Creative Types".

---

## S-TIER (unicorn scaling — mesti buat)

1. **Founder content** — cerita kenapa brand wujud, apa masalah dia nak selesaikan. #1 top performer regardless production quality (hi-fi atau lo-fi phone). Cara paling pantas connect dengan audience.
2. **Partnership ads** (creator whitelisting) — "kalau tak buat, kau bawa butter knife ke gunfight." Andromeda algorithm = persona-based, jadi reach net-new audience melalui creator yang persona kau dah engage. #1 investment untuk Meta sekarang.
3. **VSL (Video Sales Letter)** — top tier untuk health & wellness, fitness, apa-apa yang perlu banyak upfront education. Bangun trust + convert.

## A-TIER

- **Authority ads** — doctor/dermatologist (hyper-competitive niche: supplements, beauty). Higher-level validation untuk lepas objection.
- **Amateur investigation** — creator buat "investigation" pada produk/niche.
- **Yapper ads** — creator yap to camera, personal storytelling. Susah nak cari creator yang betul, tapi scale bila kena.
- **David vs Goliath** — position brand sebagai David lawan industri besar. Storytelling buat orang root for brand.
- **Grid statics** — multiple SKU/bundle, low-hanging fruit.
- **Green screen commentary** — combine dengan educational angle.
- **Catalog / DPA ads** — boleh top-of-funnel (cold) kalau setup betul.

## B-TIER (supporting cast — jangan jadi hero)

- Callout ads
- Before/After
- Progression ads (same rank as before/after — mid-funnel convert)
- Us vs Them
- Objection-handling (top 15 performer, tapi bukan scaling unicorn)
- Founder's letter static (crank masa sale period)
- Headline ads (crank kalau messaging power + combine callout)
- Educational infographic (masquerade sebagai edukasi, bukan ad)
- Mood board (apparel-specific)
- Comment reply ads (middle/lower funnel)
- Challenging your beliefs (B-roll + limiting beliefs persona)
- Hot girl Instagram stories (selfie/flat-lay/mirror)
- Creator lo-fi statics
- Conversation ads (susah execute, tapi perform bila betul)
- Tweet/Reddit ads (bagus untuk spend $100k–250k; first-frame = B)
- EGC (employee-generated content)
- TikTok love letter
- Real shorts

## C-TIER

- **AI animation ads (Pixar/claymation)** — grab net-new attention awal tapi JARANG jadi long-term winner. Operationally complex. ⚠️ ini pipeline faceless animation Fazir.
- **UGC mass** (problem-solution + voiceover + b-roll) — fatigue, volume tinggi tapi sikit yang hit.
- AI avatar ads (boleh jadi top winner, tapi trend nak phase out; brand besar makin pilih creator)
- Celebrity ads
- Warehouse ads
- Street interview ads (lebih baik fake dari real)
- Duet reaction/stitch (operationally complex — perlu rights)
- ASMR ads (crush untuk pet/beauty, tapi niche creator)

## D-TIER

- Breaking news ads
- AI billboard ads (overdone, cheesy)
- Get-ready-with-me / day in my life (tak scale paid)

## E-TIER

- Text-only ads (kecuali founder's letter masa sale)
- Listicles
- Carousel ads
- Testimonial statics (kebanyakan orang buat teruk)

## F-TIER (buang / jangan buang budget)

- **Press ads** — masalah rights/permission, publication akan datang cari.
- **Podcast ads** — waste of time sekarang.
- **Testimonial statics** (majoriti cara orang buat = F)
- **Notes app / UX ads** — confuse algorithm, tak convert.

---

## Mapping ke Fazir (faceless affiliate — supplement/weight loss/hair growth/skincare/beauty/pets)

1. **Animation (pipeline sekarang) = C-tier.** Bagus untuk TOF hook/grab attention, tapi bukan enjin scaling. Treat sebagai supporting cast, bukan hero.
2. **Enjin sebenar untuk niche health/supplement = VSL + Authority + Partnership.** VSL = faceless-friendly (voiceover + b-roll + text + before/after). Authority = boleh guna doctor/dermatologist angle (walaupun faceless brand, boleh hire creator authority).
3. **Founder story** boleh diadaptasi faceless — founder's letter (B) + founder storytelling voiceover, walaupun takde muka.
4. **Testimonial statics** = F kalau buat "slap testimonial on static" — jangan.
