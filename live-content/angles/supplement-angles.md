# Supplement Angles — Master Breakdown
*Fazir Agency | Copywriter | June 2026*

---

## Angle 1: "The Ramai Tak Tahu" (Education Angle)

- **Product:** Weight loss / General supplement
- **Target Audience:** Cold traffic, women 25–40, problem-aware but not solution-aware
- **Core Promise:** "Ada satu benda yang korang tak masukkan dalam rutin — dan benda ni yang buat semua usaha korang sia-sia"
- **Hook Phrase:** "Ramai tak tahu yang [common behavior] sebenarnya [surprising fact]. Bila aku discover ni, everything changed..."
- **Proof Points:** Ingredient science simplified, reference to clinical study, visual demo
- **Platform Fit:** TikTok (best), Facebook (good for longer explanation)
- **Variations:**
  1. "Ramai tak tahu kenapa diet korang still fail..."
  2. "Korang tau tak [ingredient] adalah kunci untuk [benefit]? Tapi 90% supplement kat Malaysia tak ada..."
  3. "Doktor sendiri cakap — tapi ramai still buat salah..."
- **Test Results:** Pending

## Angle 2: "Aku Dah Try Semua" (Relatability Angle)

- **Product:** Weight loss / Beauty supplement
- **Target Audience:** Women 25–45, tried multiple failed solutions, skeptical
- **Core Promise:** "Aku macam korang — dah try semua. Sampai jumpa benda ni."
- **Hook Phrase:** "Aku dah try detox tea, kopi kurus, intermittent fasting, meal replacement — semua. Ni satu-satunya yang bagi result..."
- **Proof Points:** Personal story arc, specific timeline, measurable result (kg/cm/inches)
- **Platform Fit:** TikTok (UGC-style), Facebook (testimonial style)
- **Variations:**
  1. Face + text overlay: list of failed attempts → fade to "sampai jumpa [product]"
  2. Green screen with old photos vs current
  3. "Aku spend RM2,000+ on supplements yang tak work. Ni yang actually berbaloi..."
- **Test Results:** Pending

## Angle 3: "Mak Cik / Ibu" (Family Trust Angle)

- **Product:** Joint health, energy, general wellness
- **Target Audience:** 35–55, family-oriented, trust-driven buyers
- **Core Promise:** "Jangan tunggu sampai mak/ayah kita sakit baru nak bertindak"
- **Hook Phrase:** "Mak aku dulu selalu cakap 'biasalah, dah tua'. Tapi lepas bagi supplement ni, dia boleh main dengan cucu balik..."
- **Proof Points:** Family testimonial, visual of active elderly, emotional storytelling
- **Platform Fit:** Facebook (best), WhatsApp
- **Variations:**
  1. "Hadiah paling bermakna untuk mak ayah korang..."
  2. "Bila mak cakap 'tak payah beli mahal-mahal' — tapi korang tahu dia perlukan..."
  3. "Ramai hantar mak ayah umrah/Haji — tapi pastikan diorang sihat dulu..."
- **Test Results:** Pending

## Angle 4: "Jangan Beli [Category] Kalau..." (Negative Buyers Guide)

- **Product:** Any supplement (differentiation play)
- **Target Audience:** Comparison shoppers, educated buyers
- **Core Promise:** "Kebanyakan supplement kat pasaran tak ada 3 benda ni — pastikan korang check sebelum beli"
- **Hook Phrase:** "Jangan beli supplement [category] kalau tak ada [X], [Y], dan [Z]. Ni kenapa..."
- **Proof Points:** Ingredient comparison chart, certification logos, competitor callout (unnamed)
- **Platform Fit:** Facebook (carousel ad), TikTok (list-style video), Shopee description
- **Variations:**
  1. "5 red flags bila beli supplement online..."
  2. "Kenapa supplement harga RM29 dan RM129 berbeza — dan yang mana patut korang pilih"
  3. "Checklist sebelum beli supplement — simpan ni!"
- **Test Results:** Pending

## Angle 5: "Korean/Japanese Beauty Secret" (Trend Angle)

- **Product:** Collagen, beauty supplement
- **Target Audience:** Women 18–35, beauty-conscious, influenced by K-beauty/J-beauty trends
- **Core Promise:** "Benda yang Korean girls pakai dari umur 20 — baru sampai Malaysia"
- **Hook Phrase:** "Korang perasan tak kulit Korean girls flawless kenapa? Bukan genetik — ini supplement yang diorang ambik hari-hari..."
- **Proof Points:** K-beauty trend reference, ingredient origin, visual of Korean beauty standard
- **Platform Fit:** TikTok (best)
- **Variations:**
  1. "Trending kat Korea 6 bulan — baru available kat Malaysia"
  2. "Apa yang Korean celebrities guna untuk glowing skin..."
  3. Green screen: "ni routine Korean girl vs routine kita"
- **Test Results:** Pending

## Angle 6: "Lepas Bersalin / Post-Partum" (Life Stage Angle)

- **Product:** Weight loss, hair growth, energy
- **Target Audience:** New mothers, 25–40, struggling with post-partum body changes
- **Core Promise:** "Badan lepas bersalin memang lain — tapi tak bermakna kena accept je"
- **Hook Phrase:** "Lepas bersalin, berat naik, rambut gugur, tenaga drop. Semua mommies experience — tapi ada cara nak bounce back..."
- **Proof Points:** Post-partum timeline, gentle/natural ingredient emphasis, breastfeeding-safe mention
- **Platform Fit:** Facebook (mom groups), TikTok (mom content)
- **Variations:**
  1. "2 bulan lepas bersalin, aku mula ambik supplement ni..."
  2. "Mommy-mommy yang struggle nak turunkan berat lepas pantang..."
  3. "Breastfeeding-friendly supplement untuk mommies — finally!"
