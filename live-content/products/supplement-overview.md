# Supplement — Market Overview (Malaysia)
- **Category:** Health & Wellness Supplements
- **Market Size:** RM 4.5B+ (Malaysia supplement market, growing 8% YoY)
- **Commission Range:** 10–40% (affiliate), 15–25% (Shopee/Lazada affiliate)
- **Top Niches by Volume:**
  1. Weight loss / Pelangsing (highest volume, most competitive)
  2. Collagen / Beauty supplements (women 20–45, high AOV)
  3. Energy / Stamina (men 25–55, high repeat)
  4. Joint health / Sendi (40+, trust-dependent)
  5. Brain / Memory / Focus (students + professionals)
  6. Immune boosters (post-COVID wave — declining but still relevant)
  7. Hair growth / Anti-hair loss (men + women)
  8. Kids supplements (vitamins, probiotics)
- **Top Marketplaces:** Shopee MY, Lazada MY, TikTok Shop MY, Watson's Online, Guardian Online
- **Key Trust Signals:** KKM registration, JAKIM Halal cert, GMP certification, clinical study mention
- **Price Anchors:**
  - Mass market: RM29–RM69/month
  - Mid-range: RM79–RM149/month
  - Premium: RM150–RM300+/month
- **Seasonal Peaks:** Pre-Raya, pre-CNY, post-Raya detox, gaji time (25th–1st)
- **Competitor Landscape:**
  - Legacy: Kinohimitsu, BElixz, NH Colla Plus, Total Image
  - DTC Challengers: Gluabika, Youvit, various TikTok Shop brands
  - Pharmacy Brands: Blackmores, Swisse, Bio-Life, 21st Century
  - MLM Crossovers: Shaklee, Herbalife, Amway Nutrilite
- **Content Compliance:** Must avoid medical claims. Use "membantu", "menyokong", "mengurangkan risiko" — not "merawat", "menyembuhkan". TikTok Shop has extra scrutiny on weight loss claims.
- **Best Performing Angles (to be updated with data):** Before/after transformation, ingredient education, "kenapa produk lain tak work", mak cik testimonial
- **Notes:** This is a HIGH-COMPLIANCE niche. Always verify product has valid KKM registration before promoting. COD is crucial for mass-market supplement buyers (trust signal). Repeat purchase rate is high if product delivers — lifetime value focus is key.
